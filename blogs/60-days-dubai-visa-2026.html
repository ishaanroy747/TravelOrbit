Dubai Visa Guide

60 Days Dubai Visa 2026: New Rules & Fast Approval Guide

Dubai continues to attract Indian travellers looking for longer holidays, family visits, shopping trips and extended stays. For travellers who need more time than a standard short visit, a 60-day option can provide greater flexibility for planning the trip.

Quick overview: The 60 Days Dubai Visa is a tourist visa option for eligible travellers who want to stay in Dubai or the UAE for an extended period. Requirements, permitted stay and entry conditions depend on the visa issued.

What Is a 60 Days Dubai Visa?

A 60-day Dubai tourist visa is designed for eligible visitors who want to stay in the UAE for an extended period for tourism.

The important point is to distinguish visa validity from permitted stay. The reference service page states that its 60-day visa remains valid for 60 days from the date of issue and provides up to 60 days of stay.

GDRFA Dubai separately lists a 60-day single-entry tourist visa and states that the visitor is granted entry for tourism for a period of 60 days.

The exact conditions printed on your approved visa should always be checked before travelling.

What Are the 2026 Dubai Visa Rules?

Dubai's visa requirements can change, so travellers should rely on current immigration information rather than older blog posts or social-media claims.

For the current GDRFA Dubai tourist visa service, key requirements include:

  • A valid passport or travel document
  • Passport validity of at least six months
  • A personal photograph
  • A ticket to continue the journey or leave the UAE
  • Valid health insurance in the UAE

GDRFA currently lists the 60-day single-entry tourist visa with a government service processing time of approximately 48 hours.

The final approval remains with the UAE immigration authorities. A visa service provider can assist with the application, but it cannot guarantee approval.

Documents Required for a 60 Days Dubai Visa

Having the right documents ready before submitting an application can reduce avoidable delays.

The reference service page currently asks applicants to provide:

  • Passport-size photograph
  • Front page of the passport
  • Last page of the passport
  • Previous Dubai exit-stamp page, if applicable
  • Confirmed air ticket
  • Hotel voucher

It also states that the passport should have at least six months of validity and that submitted scans should be clear and readable.

The exact document requirements can vary depending on the applicant and visa channel. Therefore, always check the latest checklist before submission.

How to Apply for a 60 Days Dubai Visa

The application process is generally straightforward when all information is accurate.

Step 1: Choose the Correct Visa

First, confirm that a 60-day tourist visa matches your travel plans. Also check whether you need single-entry or multiple-entry permission.

GDRFA currently provides separate services for single-entry and multiple-entry tourist visas.

Step 2: Prepare Your Documents

Keep your passport copy, photograph, travel details, accommodation information and other requested documents ready.

Make sure the spelling of your name, passport number and date of birth are consistent across your documents.

Step 3: Submit the Application

The application can be submitted through the applicable visa channel.

The UAE government confirms that online visa and entry-permit applications can be handled through official channels such as GDRFA and ICP, depending on the emirate and visa type.

Step 4: Complete Payment

Applicable government and service charges must be paid according to the selected application channel.

Do not assume that a visa-service website's total price represents the government visa fee alone.

Step 5: Wait for Processing

Processing time depends on the application and channel.

GDRFA's current service listing gives an expected completion time of 48 hours for its 60-day single-entry tourist visa service.

The reference service page, meanwhile, currently advertises a 3–4 working day processing period for its assisted application service.

These are not contradictory if understood correctly: government processing time and a private service provider's overall turnaround can be different.

Step 6: Check Your Approved Visa

Once you receive the visa, carefully check:

  • Full name
  • Passport number
  • Date of birth
  • Visa type
  • Entry conditions
  • Issue date
  • Validity
  • Permitted stay

If you identify an error, contact the relevant visa service immediately rather than waiting until the day of travel.

How Fast Can You Get a 60 Days Dubai Visa?

“Fast approval” does not mean guaranteed approval.

The current GDRFA service page lists an expected completion time of 48 hours for its 60-day single-entry tourist visa. The referenced private service currently states 3–4 working days for its application process.

Actual timelines can vary because applications may require additional checks, corrections or supporting documents.

For this reason, it is better to apply before your travel date instead of depending on last-minute processing.

Single Entry vs Multiple Entry

Travellers should also understand the difference between single-entry and multiple-entry options.

A 60-day single-entry visa is intended for one entry into the UAE, with the permitted stay determined by the visa issued.

A 60-day multiple-entry tourist visa allows multiple entries under the conditions of that visa. GDRFA currently lists a separate multiple-entry tourist visa service for 30- and 60-day options.

The right choice depends on whether you plan to enter the UAE once or leave and return during your travel period.

What Can Cause a Dubai Visa Application to Be Delayed or Rejected?

There is no guaranteed approval route, but travellers can reduce avoidable problems by submitting accurate and complete information.

Common issues include:

  • Incorrect passport information
  • Poor-quality document scans
  • Missing supporting documents
  • Passport validity that does not meet the requirement
  • Previous immigration or visa violations
  • Inconsistent personal or travel information
  • Additional checks required by immigration authorities

The reference service page specifically highlights incorrect information, passport requirements, travel-history issues and insufficient financial evidence among possible rejection factors.

A previous visa refusal does not automatically mean a new application will be rejected, but applicants should ensure that their new application is accurate and complete.

Can You Extend a 60-Day Dubai Visa?

Extension rules depend on the visa type, immigration status and the applicable UAE regulations at the time.

The reference service page states that extension assistance may be available for eligible travellers, including airport-to-airport and in-country options.

However, an extension should never be assumed to be automatic. Travellers should confirm their eligibility and extension conditions before the original visa expires.

Important: Do not overstay. Always check the expiry and permitted-stay dates on your visa.

How Dubai Visit Visa Assistance Can Help

Applying independently is possible when you understand the requirements and have the necessary documents. However, some travellers prefer professional support for document checking, application submission and status updates.

Dubai Visit Visa services can help organise the application process and reduce common documentation mistakes.

The reference service page states that its team assists with document verification and submits applications through the applicable immigration channel.

This type of assistance can be especially useful for first-time applicants or travellers who are unsure which documents are required.

Still, professional assistance should not be confused with an approval guarantee. The final decision belongs to UAE immigration authorities.

Things to Check Before Flying to Dubai

Getting your visa approved is only one part of preparing for your trip.

Before departure, make sure you:

  • Check your visa details carefully
  • Carry your passport with sufficient validity
  • Keep your approved visa accessible
  • Carry your return or onward ticket
  • Keep accommodation details available
  • Have valid UAE health insurance
  • Keep copies of important travel documents
  • Check the latest UAE entry requirements before departure

GDRFA's current tourist visa requirements specifically include a departure/onward ticket and valid health insurance.

Is a 60-Day Dubai Visa Right for You?

A 60-day option can make sense if you want to spend significantly more time in Dubai or travel around the UAE without being restricted to a short holiday.

It may suit:

  • Extended family holidays
  • Long leisure trips
  • Travellers visiting friends or relatives
  • Repeat activities and sightseeing
  • Travellers combining Dubai with other UAE destinations
  • People who want more flexibility than a shorter tourist visa

However, the best visa depends on your nationality, travel purpose, entry requirements and planned length of stay.

Final Thoughts

The 60 Days Dubai Visa can be a practical option for travellers who need a longer tourist stay in the UAE. In 2026, the key points to focus on are passport validity, travel tickets, health insurance, accurate documentation and the conditions attached to the visa issued.

Current GDRFA information confirms that 60-day tourist visas are available and that applications can be handled through digital channels, while the referenced Dubai Visit Visa service provides additional assistance with document preparation and application submission.

Rather than relying only on claims of “fast approval,” focus on submitting a complete and accurate application well before your planned departure. That approach gives you the best chance of avoiding unnecessary delays while keeping your Dubai travel plans on track.
